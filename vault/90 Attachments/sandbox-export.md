## Riddled's notes

> Photosynthesis happens in mitochondria, where sunlight turns water and carbon dioxide into glucose.

**Riddled:** Photosynthesis happens in chloroplasts, not mitochondria; it uses light energy to make sugars from water and carbon dioxide, releasing oxygen. Mitochondria release usable energy from sugars through cellular respiration.
