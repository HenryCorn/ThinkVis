## Evidence rules
**Observed** means reproduced or directly visible; **inferred** means a hypothesis supported by evidence; **proposed** means our own design. Visible controls do not establish that the feature works or is available on the free tier. Record exact limitations; do not imply recovery of private code, prompts, or models.

## Guardrails
Only inspect the authorized Riddled origin and disposable research notes. Never purchase, share publicly, bypass plan limits, expose credentials, or change existing personal notes. AI test requests may consume the free allowance; avoid repetitions.
